Hi, I'm The Grand

I am the head and heart behind The Grand Lucknow and hosting is what I love most. I've enjoyed having guests for as long as I can remember. What I grandeur about is simple: that you walk in and feel looked after. My venues aren't staged. They're warm, lively homes you can truly settle into, where comfort always comes first. I fill them with timeless pieces and special finds that carry a story of their own, and that give each venue its your character. I look after my guests personally and with real joy, before, during and after your stay.
